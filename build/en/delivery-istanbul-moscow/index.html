Cargo from Istanbul to Moscow

Direct Istanbul → Moscow cargo route. Three tariffs from $4 per kg: road and air. Our own warehouses on both sides, customs clearance included.

About the route

Istanbul and Moscow are the backbone of our network. The Istanbul office receives goods from suppliers, consolidates batches, and dispatches daily to Russia. Arriving cargo lands at the Moscow warehouse at ул. Южнопортовая 7а, стр 2, склад 8, ворота 1.

Stages

  1. Pickup at the RENEXPRESS Istanbul office, packing and weighing.
  2. Consolidation with other cargo on the same tariff.
  3. Departure by truck or air based on the selected option.
  4. Customs clearance on import to Russia.
  5. Handover at the Moscow warehouse (Yuzhnoportovaya 7a) or courier delivery.

FAQ

How long does Istanbul to Moscow delivery take?

Depends on tariff: road 14-18 days, air U3 4-5 days, air EX MARKA 3-4 days.

Where do I pick up cargo in Moscow?

From the warehouse: ул. Южнопортовая 7а, стр 2, склад 8, ворота 1. Open Mon-Fri 09:00-18:00.

Can I get courier delivery inside Moscow?

Yes, available on request — arranged with your manager separately at shipping time.

What is the minimum shipment weight?

Minimum 10 kg per shipment, no upper weight limit.
